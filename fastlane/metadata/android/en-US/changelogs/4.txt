Version 1.1.0 (Build 4)

Improvements:
- Volume controls now visible on locked devices
- Floating Button displays on lockscreen
- Edge Swipe displays on lockscreen
- Quick Settings Tile works on locked devices
- Simplified volume panel tile service by removing unlock wrapper
- Better accessibility for locked device scenarios

Note:
Quick Settings Tile (VolumePanelTileService) works on all lockscreen types (secure & non-secure).
Floating Button (FloatingButtonService) and Edge Swip (VolumeOverlayService) work best on non-secure lockscreens.
For secure lockscreens, use VolumePanelTileService quick tile for volume control.
