### $\textcolor{#D00000}{\textsf{Quick Workflow}}$

To ensure a smooth contribution process, please follow these steps:

1.  **Fork & Branch**: Create a feature branch from the `main` branch.
2.  **Focused Changes**: Keep your modifications concise and focused on a single issue or feature.
3.  **Local Verification**: Thoroughly test your changes on a physical device or emulator.
4.  **Submit PR**: Open a Pull Request on GitHub with a comprehensive summary of your work.

---

### $\textcolor{#D00000}{\textsf{Scope & Guidelines}}$

- **Atomicity**: Keep PRs small and easily reviewable.
- **Cleanliness**: Avoid mixing unrelated refactors with functional changes.
- **Language**: All user-facing strings and documentation must remain in **English**.
- **Documentation**: Update relevant `.md` files in the `docs/` folder if your changes affect user behavior or setup.

---

### $\textcolor{#D00000}{\textsf{Feature Development}}$

If you are planning to add a new feature, please refer to our technical guide:
[**`docs/add-feature.md`**](docs/add-feature.md)

---

### $\textcolor{#D00000}{\textsf{Pull Request Requirements}}$

When opening a PR, please include the following details in the description:

- **What**: A clear explanation of the changes.
- **Why**: The rationale behind the modification or the problem it solves.
- **How**: A brief description of how the changes were validated.
- **Limitations**: Any known side effects or areas for future improvement.

---

### $\textcolor{#D00000}{\textsf{Code Style}}$

- Adhere to the existing **Kotlin** and **XML** patterns established in the repository.
- Prioritize **readability** and clear naming conventions over complex abstractions.
- Ensure feature logic is strictly contained within its respective package under `io.github.rajnishkmehta.dhwanicontrol.features`.
