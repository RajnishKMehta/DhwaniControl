# $\textcolor{#0078D4}{\textsf{Privacy Policy}}$

![Version](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/RajnishKMehta/DhwaniControl?include_prereleases&style=for-the-badge)

Your privacy is paramount. **DhwaniControl** is designed with a "privacy-first" philosophy, ensuring that your data remains yours and yours alone.

---

## $\textcolor{#28A745}{\textsf{1. Information Collection and Use}}$

DhwaniControl does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or device-identifying information.
- **No Trackers:** The app contains no third-party tracking libraries or analytics.
- **No Ads:** The app is completely ad-free.
- **No Cloud Sync:** Your settings are never uploaded to any server.

---

## $\textcolor{#6F42C1}{\textsf{2. Permissions Explained}}$

To provide its core functionality, DhwaniControl requires certain Android system permissions. Here is why they are needed:

- **Display over other apps (Overlay):** This is required to show the **Floating Button** and detect **Edge Swipe** gestures while you are using other applications.
- **Post Notifications:** On Android 13+, this permission is necessary to keep the background service active and stable, ensuring the on-screen triggers remain available when you need them.

---

## $\textcolor{#D9730D}{\textsf{3. Local Data Storage}}$

Any configuration you make within the app (such as the position of the floating button, icon styles, or edge preferences) is stored **locally** on your device. This data is never shared and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

---

## $\textcolor{#008080}{\textsf{4. Open Source Transparency}}$

We believe in full transparency. As an open-source project, our entire codebase is available for public audit. You can verify our privacy claims by reviewing the source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/RajnishKMehta/DhwaniControl).

---

## $\textcolor{#D00000}{\textsf{5. Contact}}$

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to open an issue on our GitHub repository.
