# Assist App Integration

DhwaniControl supports being set as the default Digital Assistant on Android. When invoked, it opens the system volume panel.

## Setup
1. Open the DhwaniControl app.
2. Under "Assist App", if it shows "Disabled", click on the toggle button.
3. This will open the Android Default Apps settings.
4. Select "DhwaniControl" as your Digital Assistant.
5. Now, when you invoke your assistant (via voice gesture, home button long-press, or power button depending on your phone's settings), the volume panel will open.

## Troubleshooting
If it is not working, make sure DhwaniControl is explicitly selected as your Digital Assistant in `Settings -> Apps -> Default Apps -> Digital Assistant`.
